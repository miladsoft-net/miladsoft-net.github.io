> Er Leng and A Bao didn't go home for the New Year this year. This is the first time Er Leng didn't go home for the New Year. The couple sat on the sofa in their small home in Chongqing, eating nuts, drinking juice, and laughing while watching the Spring Festival Gala.

For me, the New Year only starts after the Lunar New Year's Eve. Looking at the year-end review with only a title buried in bear, I couldn't even enjoy watching the Spring Festival Gala.

How should I put it, this year was an extraordinary yet ordinary year; extraordinary because a lot of things happened this year; Xiao Bao passed away, A Bao got pregnant, Er Leng got sick, Fu Gui Er passed away, and even my little scooter was stolen. And ordinary because no matter how many things happened to us, compared to last year and the year before, nothing was a big deal. As for the pandemic? In these chaotic times, I have nothing to say about it, and I don't care how many people got infected or passed away. As long as my family and I are healthy and safe, everything else is up to fate! I am just an ordinary person in this vast world.

## Work & Technology
Looking back at the flags I set last year, it seems that most of them were not completed. In the middle of the year, due to the company's transformation, I started learning the Go language. After using it for one or two projects, I increasingly felt the simplicity of Go language at its core. While maintaining native support for concurrency, it did not add extra features like object orientation, inheritance, or generics. Although the amount of code written might increase, the perfect balance between simplicity and efficiency is enough.

> ~~I actually don't favor generics, as they would significantly increase the complexity of the language. Keeping it simple is good.~~
> After looking at Rust, I take back my previous opinion. I now think Go should support generics sooner.

Although Go language is indeed simple, as I delve deeper into its principles, there are more and more features to learn. With deeper usage, I am now even starting to learn about computer systems. Topics like memory management, memory allocation, and compiler principles. Most programmers who have worked for a few years will probably go back to pick up the things they left behind.

This year, I open-sourced 2-3 libraries, gaining over 300 stars. After two years, I finally completed version 2.0 of OCR, even though not many people use it, it lives up to the original intention of open-sourcing it.
